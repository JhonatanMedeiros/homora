# Code of Conduct

Homora welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. We want discussions and contributions to be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and communicate in good faith. Explain disagreements without attacking people.
- Welcome questions, feedback and different levels of experience. Respect a person's decision not to share personal information.
- Respect privacy. Do not post someone else's private information, credentials or infrastructure details without their permission.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks and publication of private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, ask someone to stop, or restrict participation when needed. For a sensitive conduct report, contact a maintainer privately using a contact method listed on their GitHub profile if available. Do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue. The project does not yet have a dedicated conduct-reporting address or guaranteed response time.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. This code applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests and other spaces operated for Homora.
